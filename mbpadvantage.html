What no competitor can match
  • 01
    Integration
    The only true one-roof integrated platform in the world
    When MBP opened in 1999, services spanning genome engineering, cryopreservation, phenotyping, vivarium, and repository were unavailable under one roof anywhere in the world. That has not changed. No other organisation offers the full arc from CRISPR design → model production → phenotyping → colony management → biobanking → informatics under a single scientific team with a single project manager. MBP coordinates ~600 active projects per year across all five divisions simultaneously.
  • 02
    Unique Facility
    The only gnotobiotic resource center on campus
    MBP operates the only Gnotobiotic Mouse Resource Center on the UC Davis campus, with flexible film isolators, semi-rigid isolators, and individually ventilated isolated cages for germ-free and gnotobiotic mice. No commercial CRO offers gnotobiotic services as a standard offering. This is an essential, unbridgeable gap for any serious microbiome, immune reconstitution, or colonisation research programme.
  • 03
    Biosafety
    ABSL3 access — infectious disease in live mouse models
    MBP has a formal MOU giving it access to the campus ABSL3 facility — a Cornerstone-certified, fully-contained concrete facility. This means MBP can work with BSL3 pathogens in live mouse models. No commercial CRO routinely offers this. It is essential for infectious disease research including HIV, emerging pathogens, and vaccine development studies where animal-level biosafety is non-negotiable.
  • 04
    National Designation
    MMRRC — the largest federally designated national bioarchive
    MBP hosts the largest of the four national Mutant Mouse Resource and Research Centers — a federally designated national bioarchive housing thousands of mouse strains. This is not a capability that can be purchased or built. It is a 25-year institutional designation that carries access to strains, scientific credibility, and NIH visibility that no commercial company has. The MMRRC is a unique scientific asset available exclusively through MBP.
  • 05
    First Mover
    One of the first academic NAM Testing Centers in the United States
    MBP is positioned as one of the first academic NAM (New Approach Methodology) Testing Centers in the US — offering NAM validation with the academic rigor and regulatory-grade data packages that FDA submission requires. No standard CRO is positioned as a NAM validation centre with this level of institutional credibility. This is an entirely new service category where MBP has first-mover status and unmatched infrastructure for bridging in vitro findings to in vivo benchmarks.
  • 06
    Scientific Authorship
    Staff who originate techniques — not just execute them
    MBP scientists actively develop and publish new approaches — CRISPR-EZ, zygote electroporation protocols, oocyte vitrification — that then become standard services adopted across the field. At most facilities, scientists execute protocols. At MBP, scientists invent them. The scientific literature treats MBP protocols as reference standards. That credibility cannot be bought or contracted.
  • 07
    Proof of Capability
    The HIV mouse model — doing what others cannot
    MBP generated a mouse model that can be infected with HIV — something described at the time as "impossible to achieve until now." This required combining CRISPR editing of multiple human receptor genes with complex, multi-generational breeding strategies. No commercial CRO has successfully done this. It demonstrates that MBP takes on projects that others decline or genuinely cannot execute, and delivers.
  • 08
    Data Infrastructure
    Bespoke informatics — millions of data points, full traceability
    MBP has an in-house division of four programmers maintaining a custom LIMS tracking millions of data points annually across 40+ procedures. Where most organisations rely on off-the-shelf platforms, MBP's bespoke system supports data traceability, reproducibility documentation, and audit trails at a level that matters for regulatory submissions, GLP-adjacent work, and any study where scientific defensibility is required.
  • 09
    Third-Party Validation
    285 publications and 120 NIH grants — independently verified quality
    Over five years, MBP services have supported 285 peer-reviewed publications and 120 NIH grants — a scale unmatched by any single core facility. Each publication that cites MBP services is an independent, third-party validation of the scientific quality of the output — the kind of track record that matters when a regulatory submission or grant renewal depends on reproducible, publication-grade data.
  • 10
    Institutional Embedding
    NCI-rated "exceptional" — UC Davis Comprehensive Cancer Center
    MBP is formally embedded in the UC Davis Comprehensive Cancer Center as the Mouse Biology Shared Resource with an NCI rating of "exceptional" — the highest possible. Cancer Center members receive subsidised rates through the CCSG. This creates a scientifically credible oncology pipeline, access to Cancer Center investigators, and institutional accountability that a commercial CRO cannot replicate.
