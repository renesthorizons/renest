Guarantee language (industry framing)

A guaranteed deliverable means mice with the specified genetic modification will be produced or the project remediated accordingly. It does not and cannot guarantee a particular phenotypic outcome. Vendor guarantees described in market materials typically cover genotype delivery, not phenotype.
